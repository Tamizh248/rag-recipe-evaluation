RECIPE: Rosemary Olive Focaccia (1.5kg)

Yields one 1.5kg tray focaccia (approximately 33cm x 23cm).

INGREDIENTS:
Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage
Bread Flour | 800g | 100%
Water | 600g | 75%
Fine Sea Salt | 16g | 2%
Active Sourdough Starter | 160g | 20%
Extra Virgin Olive Oil | 80g | 10%
Pitted Kalamata Olives | 100g | 12.5%
Fresh Rosemary | 6g | 0.75%

METHOD:
1. Mix the bread flour, water, and active sourdough starter into a shaggy dough, then rest for 20 minutes.
2. Add the fine sea salt and half of the olive oil, mixing until smooth and elastic.
3. Bulk ferment for 3 to 4 hours, performing an oiled stretch-and-fold every 30 minutes for the first 90 minutes.
4. Pour the remaining olive oil into a rimmed baking tray and transfer the dough onto it, gently stretching it toward the edges.
5. Proof uncovered for 45 to 60 minutes, then dimple the surface firmly with oiled fingers.
6. Press the pitted Kalamata olives and fresh rosemary into the dimples across the surface.
7. Bake at 220C (425F) for 22-25 minutes until deeply golden.
8. Cool in the tray for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

ALLERGENS:
Contains gluten (wheat). Produced in a facility that also processes tree nuts. Contains no dairy or egg.
