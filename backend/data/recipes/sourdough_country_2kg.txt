RECIPE: Sourdough Country Loaf (2kg)

Yields approximately 1970g of dough, enough for two 1kg boules.

INGREDIENTS:
Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage
Bread Flour | 1000g | 100%
Water | 750g | 75%
Fine Sea Salt | 20g | 2%
Active Sourdough Starter | 200g | 20%

METHOD:
1. Mix the bread flour and water until no dry flour remains, then rest (autolyse) for 45 minutes.
2. Add the active sourdough starter and the fine sea salt. Mix thoroughly until fully incorporated.
3. Bulk ferment for 4 to 5 hours at room temperature, performing a set of stretch-and-folds every 30 minutes for the first 2 hours.
4. Once the dough has risen by about 50% and feels airy, divide it into two pieces and pre-shape each into a round.
5. Rest the pre-shaped rounds for 20 minutes, then shape each firmly into a boule and place seam-side up in a floured banneton.
6. Cold proof the shaped loaves in the refrigerator for 12 to 16 hours.
7. Preheat a covered Dutch oven to 250C (480F). Score the cold loaf and bake covered for 20 minutes, then uncovered for a further 20-25 minutes until deeply browned.
8. Cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour before slicing.

ALLERGENS:
Contains gluten (wheat). Produced in a facility that also processes tree nuts. Contains no dairy, egg, or soy.
