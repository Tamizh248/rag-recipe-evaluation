RECIPE: Sourdough Brioche Loaf (900g)

Yields approximately 1060g of dough, enough for one 900g pan loaf.

INGREDIENTS:
Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage
Bread Flour | 500g | 100%
Whole Milk | 150g | 30%
Eggs (whole) | 100g | 20%
Unsalted Butter | 150g | 30%
Granulated Sugar | 50g | 10%
Fine Sea Salt | 10g | 2%
Active Sourdough Starter | 100g | 20%

METHOD:
1. Combine the bread flour, whole milk, eggs, granulated sugar, and active sourdough starter, and mix on low speed until a rough dough forms.
2. Increase speed and mix until the dough begins to clear the bowl, about 8-10 minutes, then add the fine sea salt.
3. Add the unsalted butter a few pieces at a time, mixing after each addition until fully absorbed, until the dough passes the windowpane test.
4. Bulk ferment at room temperature for 1 hour, then refrigerate the covered dough to bulk retard overnight for 10 to 12 hours.
5. The next day, turn out the chilled dough, shape into a loaf, and place in a buttered loaf pan.
6. Proof at room temperature for 3 to 4 hours until the dough crowns above the rim of the pan.
7. Brush with egg wash and bake at 175C (350F) for 35-40 minutes until deep golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 90C (195F).
8. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.

ALLERGENS:
Contains gluten (wheat), dairy (whole milk, butter), and eggs. Contains no tree nuts.
