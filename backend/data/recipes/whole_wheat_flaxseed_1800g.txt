RECIPE: Whole Wheat Flaxseed Sourdough (1.8kg)

Yields approximately 2080g of dough, enough for two 900g boules (about 1.8kg total).

INGREDIENTS:
Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage
Whole Wheat Flour | 1000g | 100%
Water | 800g | 80%
Fine Sea Salt | 20g | 2%
Active Sourdough Starter | 200g | 20%
Flaxseed (ground) | 60g | 6%

METHOD:
1. Mix the whole wheat flour and water and rest (autolyse) for 60 minutes; whole wheat bran needs longer hydration than white flour.
2. Add the active sourdough starter, fine sea salt, and ground flaxseed, mixing until fully incorporated.
3. Bulk ferment for 4 to 5 hours at room temperature, performing a stretch-and-fold every 30 minutes for the first 2 hours since whole wheat dough is less extensible.
4. Divide the dough into two pieces, pre-shape into rounds, and rest for 20 minutes.
5. Shape each piece into a boule and place seam-side up in a floured banneton.
6. Cold proof in the refrigerator for 12 to 14 hours.
7. Preheat a covered Dutch oven to 240C (465F). Score and bake covered for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 20-25 minutes until deeply browned.
8. Cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour before slicing.

ALLERGENS:
Contains gluten (wheat) and flaxseed. Contains no dairy, egg, or tree nuts.
