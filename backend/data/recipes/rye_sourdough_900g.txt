RECIPE: Caraway Rye Sourdough (900g)

Yields approximately 1075g of dough, enough for one 900g batard.

INGREDIENTS:
Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage
Dark Rye Flour | 500g | 100%
Water | 400g | 80%
Fine Sea Salt | 10g | 2%
Active Rye Starter | 150g | 30%
Caraway Seeds | 15g | 3%

METHOD:
1. Combine the dark rye flour and water and rest (autolyse) for 30 minutes.
2. Add the fine sea salt after the autolyse, then mix in the active rye starter and caraway seeds until evenly distributed.
3. Bulk ferment for 2.5 to 3 hours at room temperature. Rye dough ferments faster than wheat dough and needs only one gentle fold at the 45-minute mark.
4. Once the dough has visibly loosened and domed, turn it out and shape gently into a batard, taking care not to degas it too much since rye doughs are stickier and less elastic.
5. Proof seam-side up in a floured banneton for 45 to 60 minutes at room temperature.
6. Preheat the oven with a baking stone to 230C (450F). Bake for 40-45 minutes until the crust is deep brown and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped.
7. Cool completely, at least 2 hours, before slicing since rye crumb continues to set as it cools.

ALLERGENS:
Contains gluten (rye). Contains no dairy, egg, or tree nuts.
